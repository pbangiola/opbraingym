Project class previews

Make your own cutting board

Turn a piece of hardwood into something you’ll use every day. Start with a simple design, work with the grain, and shape and finish a board that feels good in your hands.

What you’ll learn

  • Choose stock and sketch a board with useful dimensions.
  • Measure and lay out your work; follow a sequence for cutting, surfacing and shaping.
  • Sand, ease the edges, and choose an appropriate finish and care routine.

Project: a simple hardwood cutting board, with Paul’s walnut boards as examples.

Beginner project. Tool use and machine operations are assigned by the instructor to suit each participant; some steps may be instructor demonstrations. Family participation and supervision requirements will be confirmed before booking.

Small woodcarving 101

Find a shape inside a small block of wood. Learn to read the grain, make controlled cuts, and use a few simple forms to give a piece its own character.

What you’ll learn

  • Choose a suitable blank and sketch a simple design.
  • Learn safe hand positions, work holding, tool handling and basic carving cuts.
  • Refine the shape and decide which surfaces to smooth and which tool marks to keep.

Project: a small carved form or character. Paul’s chess kings show how a simple block can become an individual piece.

Beginner introduction. The instructor will confirm age suitability, supervision and tool choice before participation; carving uses sharp tools.
