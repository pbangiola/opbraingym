Test on a hidden area and follow the named product’s directions. Cleaners can damage finishes and plastics. Sand only where removing surface material is acceptable. Ventilate, keep solvents away from ignition sources, and never heat solvent-wet work. This chart covers tools and surfaces, not skin. Rinse food-contact surfaces as directed.

Cleaner and formulation notes

Degreasers and all-purpose cleaners are product-specific formulations. ² Soap and water is documented for uncured Gorilla Epoxy, not all epoxies. ³ Original Gorilla Glue specifies standard paint thinner; blends vary. Do not substitute an arbitrary thinner. Wet + dry indicates both states are documented; loosening is not necessarily dissolution.

Material notes and sources

  1. Silly Putty — On fabric. Lift bulk with a dull knife or spoon. Blot with alcohol, rinse, then use dish soap for a remaining stain. Crayola — Silly Putty on fabric
  2. Silly Putty — On hard plastic. Use a compatible all-purpose cleaner on residue. The fabric alcohol method is not automatically suitable for plastic. Crayola — Silly Putty on hard plastic
  3. Chewing gum — On carpet. Hold a sealed ice pack on gum for 3–5 minutes, then gently lift hardened gum. Ice is a mechanical aid, not a solvent. BISSELL — Removing gum from carpet
  4. Cooking oil & fresh grease — Oil, butter, food fats. Remove the bulk, wash with dish detergent and water, then rinse food-contact items. Dawn — Dish detergent and kitchen grease
  5. Sticky cooking grease — Compatible kitchen surfaces. Use a kitchen degreaser labeled for the surface. Dawn Powerwash lists counters, stoves and grills; check the appliance instructions too. Dawn — Powerwash uses
  6. Motor oil & automotive grease — Washable vehicle parts / tools. Simple Green MAX specifically targets automotive oil and grease. Follow its dilution, compatibility and rinsing directions. Simple Green — MAX automotive degreaser
  7. Heavy machine grease — Cutting fluids, oily grime. Use an industrial degreaser on compatible washable surfaces. Do not flood assembled motors, bearings or electrical parts. Simple Green — Industrial cleaner and dilution guidance
  8. Tape & label adhesive — Non-curing sticky residue. 3M Citrus Base is documented for tape, label and sticker residue. Use the remover’s surface restrictions. 3M — Citrus Base adhesive remover
  9. Hot-melt glue — Cooled craft glue. Work rubbing alcohol around the edges, then lift. This releases the bond rather than necessarily dissolving the blob. Loctite — Removing hot glue
  10. Wood glue — Titebond Original, II, III. Clean wet squeeze-out with a damp cloth. After drying, scrape or sand. Water cleanup is not a claim of cured water-solubility. Titebond — Wood glue and hide glue FAQs
  11. Liquid hide glue — Titebond Liquid Hide Glue. Water absorbed at the edge can weaken a dried joint. This describes bond loosening, not instant dissolution. Titebond — Wood glue and hide glue FAQs
  12. Super glue / CA — Gorilla Super Glue. Warm soapy soaking can help release cured glue; acetone is also documented. IPA is listed for uncured cleanup. Gorilla — Super Glue cleanup · Gorilla — Super Glue Gel cured-bond removal
  13. Two-part epoxy — Gorilla Epoxy. Gorilla lists soap and water, IPA or acetone while uncured. Cured epoxy resists ordinary cleanup solvents; controlled heat can assist scraping. Never heat solvent-wet work. Gorilla — Epoxy cleanup · System Three — Cured and uncured epoxy removal
  14. Foaming polyurethane glue — Original Gorilla Glue. For wet squeeze-out, Gorilla specifies a dry cloth or standard paint thinner. Once dry, remove mechanically. Gorilla — Original polyurethane glue
  15. PU construction adhesive — Titebond Fast Set. Mineral spirits cleans tools and adhesive before drying; dried material needs mechanical removal. Titebond — Fast Set polyurethane construction adhesive
  16. Contact cement — DAP Weldwood Original spray. Mineral spirits is specified for excess wet adhesive. This does not establish removal of a fully dried contact bond. DAP — Weldwood Original spray adhesive technical data
  17. Flexible repair adhesive — E6000 Industrial. Original Industrial formulation: acetone or citrus-based solvent before curing; cutting and scraping afterward. Eclectic Products — E6000 Industrial
  18. Flexible repair adhesive — E6000 PLUS. PLUS has different cleanup guidance from original E6000. Do not transfer recommendations between versions. Eclectic Products — E6000 PLUS cleanup FAQs
  19. Hybrid / MS polymer sealant — Titebond WeatherMaster. IPA or acetone before curing. After curing, scrape or cut off the sealant. Titebond — Construction adhesives and sealants FAQs
  20. Silicone sealant — DAP 100% silicone. Mineral spirits is specified for uncured sealant. That does not establish that it dissolves cured silicone. DAP — 100% silicone technical data
  21. Water-based construction glue — Titebond water-based products. Titebond lists water while wet and mineral spirits after drying for its water-based construction adhesives. This is separate from wood-glue guidance. Titebond — Construction adhesives and sealants FAQs
  22. Water-based caulk — Titebond water-based caulks. Titebond lists mineral spirits or orange-based cleaners with scraping after full drying. Titebond — Construction adhesives and sealants FAQs
