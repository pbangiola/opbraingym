Equipment and access are subject to availability, orientation, and project suitability. Ask before planning a visit around a particular machine.

Tool inventory & safety requirements

These are Brain Gym access requirements. Orientation covers the shop, emergencies, PPE, cleanup and the tool’s hazards. Training means hands-on instruction and an observed demonstration before independent use; previous experience can be assessed. A class may provide that instruction under supervision. Permission applies to the specific tool and task.

For all workshop work: closed-toe shoes, secured hair and fitted clothing; remove dangling jewelry. Eyes means impact-rated safety glasses with side protection; add goggles or a face shield where the task requires them. Hearing protection is required for the noisy operations listed below, including people nearby. Do not wear gloves around exposed rotating blades, bits, spindles or rollers.

Dust and fumes: use extraction and ventilation first. The instructor assesses the material, task and controls before work; required respiratory protection must be selected and fitted appropriately, with any applicable medical evaluation and training. A dust mask does not protect against solvent vapors. Stop work if adequate controls are unavailable.

The list combines named equipment with existing inventory categories. A broad category is not approval for every tool in it. Manufacturer instructions and the instructor’s task review may add requirements. PPE does not replace guarding, workholding or safe technique.
