A field guide for the kitchen & workshop

Getting sticky
stuff off.

Glue. Gum. Putty. Grease. Start with what’s stuck, then choose a cleaner and a way to lift it away.

Manufacturer guidance checked October 25, 2026 · 23 material entries

Wet glue and cured glue are different problems.

A cleaner that handles a fresh spill may do very little after it hardens. And the method has to suit the surface underneath.

Find your material ↓

Four ways removal can work · schematic illustrations

Dissolve / soften

A compatible solvent loosens residue so you can wipe or scrape it away.

Wash away

Detergent helps carry oil and grease away with water.

Release / peel

Lift the material from the surface. Chilling can help with gum.

Abrade

Scraping and sanding remove solid material—and can remove the surface too.

“Wet + dry” means both states are documented. “Loosen dry” means gradual bond release, not necessarily dissolution. Mechanical methods range from soft wiping to destructive sanding.

Before you start

Keep the thing you’re cleaning intact.

  • Test a hidden spot. Acetone, alcohol, and other cleaners can damage plastics, coatings, paint, or finishes.
  • Use soft wiping before abrasion where the surface permits. Sanding belongs on surfaces you can afford to refinish—not appliance paint, seals, or precision bearing surfaces.
  • Follow cleaner labels and safety data sheets. Ventilate and keep flammable solvents away from ignition sources. Do not mix cleaners or heat solvent-wet work.
  • This chart covers surfaces and tools. It is not a guide for removing chemicals or glue from skin.

The KitchenAid distinction

Cleaning a KitchenAid mixer.

For the mixer’s body, KitchenAid specifies a soft damp cloth and no abrasive cleaners. Unplug before cleaning and do not immerse the mixer. [8]

Factory gearbox grease is food grade and approved for incidental food contact. [9] That does not make automotive grease, garage degreaser, or a general solvent appropriate inside a food appliance.

Internal servicing needs model-specific instructions. This chart does not prescribe a gearbox solvent or replacement lubricant. Wash and rinse food-contact items using their care instructions before returning them to use.
