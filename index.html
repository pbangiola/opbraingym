About

Do something real.
…like, today.

A lot of people want to build, repair, tinker, or learn how things work.

Then reality gets in the way.

You need the right tool. You need somewhere to use it. You need to know how not to hurt yourself. And sometimes you just need somebody nearby who knows what they’re doing.

The Brain Gym is an experiment in making all of that easier.

For now, it’s a small workshop in my home in Oak Park. I’m opening it a few hours at a time to people who want to learn, work on a project, or simply get their hands on a tool they don’t own.

The long-term idea is bigger. But this is where it starts.

On your schedule.
Not by yourself.

Come with a project, a problem, or no idea what you’re doing yet. I’ll help you get oriented, make sure you can work safely, and then give you as much—or as little—help as you need.

Before You Come

It’s a working shop.

The Brain Gym is currently a small working home shop, not a finished commercial makerspace.

Expect sawdust. Wear clothes you can work in and closed-toe shoes.

Some tools require a quick safety check before use. Some projects or tools may require supported time rather than independent shop time.

If you’re unsure whether your project is a fit, ask first.

What Brain Gym Is Becoming

This is the first version.

The long-term goal is a neighborhood workshop where people can walk in, learn real skills, use well-maintained tools and work alongside other people who like making things.

Woodworking. Electrical. Metalwork. Repair. Fabrication. And whatever else proves worth building.

But I don’t want to build the imaginary version first.

So Brain Gym is starting here, with a real shop, real projects and real people.

If people use it, we’ll grow it.
